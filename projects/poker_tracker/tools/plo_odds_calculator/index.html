PLO equity is Monte Carlo estimated (evaluating all 60 two-card / three-card combinations per hand). Equity % is your average share of the pot across all runouts — in double board, each board is worth half the pot, so winning just one board is worth ~50%. Scoop % is how often you take the whole pot outright, and Split % is how often you only get a partial share (one board, or a chopped board).
